# Cantrips, Spell Slots, and First-Level Magic

**Source:** Synthetic clip · D&D trivia · POC fixture

Spellcasting in Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition divides magic into **cantrips** (at-will) and **leveled spells** (limited by spell slots).

## Cantrips

Cantrips are 0-level spells a caster can use repeatedly without expending slots. Examples:

- **Fire Bolt** (Wizard/Sorcerer) — ranged fire damage
- **Sacred Flame** (Cleric) — radiant damage, ignores cover
- **Vicious Mockery** (Bard) — psychic damage and disadvantage on next attack
- **Eldritch Blast** (Warlock) — iconic multiple-beam scaling cantrip

Cantrips scale in damage at character levels 5, 11, and 17 for most classes.

## Spell slots

Leveled spells consume spell slots. A 1st-level Wizard with Intelligence +3 and two 1st-level slots might prepare **Burning Hands**, **Shield**, **Magic Missile**, and **Detect Magic**, but can only cast a combined total of two leveled spells before a long rest (unless using Arcane Recovery).

| Class (level 1) | Cantrips known | 1st-level slots |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wizard | 3 | 2 |
| Cleric | 3 | 2 |
| Sorcerer | 4 | 2 |
| Bard | 2 | 2 |
| Warlock | 2 | 1 (Pact Magic) |

## Ritual casting

Some spells bear the **ritual** tag. A caster with the Ritual Casting feature can cast them in 10 extra minutes without using a slot, provided the spell is prepared or on their class list.

**Trivia:** *Prestidigitation* is the cantrip most players use to clean mud off armor, warm coffee, or create a harmless sensory effect for roleplay.
