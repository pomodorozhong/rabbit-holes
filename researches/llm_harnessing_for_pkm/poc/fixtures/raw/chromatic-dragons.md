# Chromatic Dragons: Colors, Temperaments, and Lairs

**Source:** Synthetic clip · D&D trivia · POC fixture

In Dungeons & Dragons, chromatic dragons are the classic evil-aligned wyrms tied to destructive elemental temperaments. There are five primary colors, each with distinct breath weapons, personalities, and lair preferences.

## Red dragons

Red dragons are arrogant, greedy, and obsessed with treasure. They prefer volcanic mountains and scorched badlands. Their breath weapon is fire. Red dragons are among the most powerful chromatics and often rule entire regions through fear.

## Blue dragons

Blue dragons are vain and strategic. They favor deserts and arid canyons where lightning storms are common. Their breath weapon is lightning. They enjoy manipulating mortals from afar and often hoard sapphires.

## Green dragons

Green dragons are deceptive manipulators. They lurk in ancient forests, swamps, and overgrown ruins. Their breath weapon is poison gas. Green dragons excel at intrigue and rarely fight fair.

## Black dragons

Black dragons are cruel and petty. They dwell in swamps, jungles, and acidic bogs. Their breath weapon is acid. A black dragon's lair is usually a stinking morass riddled with hidden tunnels.

## White dragons

White dragons are feral and bestial, the least intelligent of the chromatics. They inhabit frozen tundra and icy peaks. Their breath weapon is cold. They hunt more than they scheme, but their size still makes them deadly.

## Quick comparison

| Color | Breath | Typical terrain | Personality |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Red | Fire | Volcanoes | Arrogant, greedy |
| Blue | Lightning | Deserts | Vain, scheming |
| Green | Poison | Forests, swamps | Deceptive |
| Black | Acid | Bogs | Cruel |
| White | Cold | Tundra | Bestial |

**Trivia:** All chromatic dragons worship Tiamat, the five-headed Dragon Queen, in most campaign settings.
