# Data Sources

Each CSV in this folder is derived from publicly available industry reports. Retrieved: **2026-07-09**.

---

## Crowdfunding

### `kickstarter_rpg_crowdfunding_yearly.csv`

All-platform RPG crowdfunding totals by year (2013–2025).

- **Source:** [2025 Year End RPG-related Crowdfunding Report](https://skalchemist.cloud/mediawiki/index.php/2025_Year_End_RPG-related_Crowdfunding_Report) (Skalchemist)
- **Notes:** Values inflation-adjusted to November 2025 USD. Includes Kickstarter, Backerkit, GameOnTabletop, and Gamefound.

### `kickstarter_tabletop_games_2024.csv`

Kickstarter tabletop games category metrics for 2024.

- **Source:** [2024 Was a Big Year for Games on Kickstarter](https://updates.kickstarter.com/kickstarter-biggest-platform-for-games/) (Kickstarter official blog)
- **Notes:** Covers all tabletop games, not RPG-only.

### `kickstarter_success_rate_yearly.csv`

Tabletop games success rate on Kickstarter (2022–2024).

- **Source:** [Kickstarter Creative Download 2024 — TTRPG Analysis](https://www.rpgdrop.com/kickstarters-the-creative-download-2024-analysis-with-focus-on-ttrpg-projects/) (RPG Drop)

### `kickstarter_top_ttrpg_2024.csv`

Top-funded TTRPG Kickstarter campaigns in 2024.

- **Source:** [Kickstarter Creative Download 2024 — TTRPG Analysis](https://www.rpgdrop.com/kickstarters-the-creative-download-2024-analysis-with-focus-on-ttrpg-projects/) (RPG Drop)

### `crowdfunding_platform_share_2025.csv`

Share of RPG crowdfunding projects and funding by platform in 2025.

- **Source:** [2025 Year End RPG-related Crowdfunding Report](https://skalchemist.cloud/mediawiki/index.php/2025_Year_End_RPG-related_Crowdfunding_Report) (Skalchemist)

### `crowdfunding_non5e_systems_2025.csv`

Non-D&D 5E RPG systems with 5 or more related crowdfunding projects in 2025.

- **Source:** [2025 Year End RPG-related Crowdfunding Report](https://skalchemist.cloud/mediawiki/index.php/2025_Year_End_RPG-related_Crowdfunding_Report) (Skalchemist)

### `crowdfunding_november_yoy.csv`

Year-over-year comparison of successful November TTRPG crowdfunding campaigns (2023–2025).

- **Source:** [November 2025 TTRPG Crowdfunding Retrospective](https://ttrpg-spider.blogspot.com/2025/12/november-2025-ttrpg-crowdfunding.html) (Web of the Gigantic Spider)
- **Notes:** Covers Kickstarter and Backerkit. Includes D&D 5E and AI-assisted campaign breakdowns.

## Market & Player Preferences

### `market_indicators.csv`

Global market size, player-base growth, genre share, and related industry estimates.

- **Source:** [Worldwide TTRPG Market in 2024 – Industry Analysis](https://www.rpgdrop.com/worldwide-ttrpg-market-in-2024-industry-analysis/) (RPG Drop)
- **Notes:** Aggregates figures from multiple industry reports (ICv2, Kickstarter tracking, market research).

### `player_engagement_wotc_2024.csv`

Official D&D and D&D Beyond engagement metrics reported by Wizards of the Coast.

- **Source:** [Wizards Of The Coast VP Reveals D&D 2024 Sales and Digital Usage](https://techraptor.net/tabletop/news/wizards-of-coast-vp-reveals-dungeons-dragons-2024-sales-and-digital-usage) (TechRaptor, reporting WotC presentation)

### `hobby_store_rpg_rankings.csv`

ICv2 hobby-channel RPG line rankings (rank order only).

- **Source:** [Worldwide TTRPG Market in 2024 – Industry Analysis](https://www.rpgdrop.com/worldwide-ttrpg-market-in-2024-industry-analysis/) (RPG Drop, citing ICv2)
- **Notes:** ICv2 publishes rank order, not dollar-percentage market share.
